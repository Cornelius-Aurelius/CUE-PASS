03 / WHY THIS MATTERS

More than colour.

A footballer can use numbers, roles, patterns and communication to make decisions when kit colours are difficult to distinguish. Using more than one visual signal helps make the game more accessible.

This is an educational game, not a colour-blindness test or a medical simulation.

THE STORY BEHIND THE SIGNAL

GOOD FOOTBALL DECISIONS NEED CLEAR CUES.

CUE PASS — Matchday Vision is an original football passing and visual-recognition game created, designed and developed by Cornelius Aurelius. It explores how shirt numbers, team positions, uniform patterns, armbands and distinctive equipment can help players recognise a passing option. The teaching concept complements the creator’s wider Colour Blindness in Football awareness project.

Scenarios are simplified training puzzles, not real match tactics or medical assessments. Guidance from The Football Association and UEFA recommends considering colour-vision accessibility when selecting kits, equipment and visual communications.
